## Chapter Overview

### Chapter 1 – The Machine Learning Landscape
Introduces fundamental Machine Learning concepts, learning paradigms, and system classifications. It discusses supervised, unsupervised, semi-supervised, and reinforcement learning, along with batch and online learning approaches. Common challenges such as overfitting, underfitting, and data quality are also addressed.

### Chapter 2 – End-to-End Machine Learning Project
Presents a complete Machine Learning workflow from problem framing to deployment. Emphasis is placed on data collection, exploratory data analysis, preprocessing, feature engineering, cross-validation, and hyperparameter tuning in real-world scenarios.

### Chapter 3 – Classification
Focuses on classification tasks and evaluation metrics. Topics include binary and multiclass classification, confusion matrices, precision, recall, F1-score, ROC curves, AUC, multilabel classification, and error analysis.

### Chapter 4 – Training Models
Explains how models are trained using optimization techniques. It covers linear and polynomial regression, gradient descent variants, learning curves, regularization methods, logistic regression, and the bias–variance trade-off.

### Chapter 5 – Support Vector Machines
Covers Support Vector Machines for classification and regression. The chapter explains margin maximization, soft margins, kernel methods, the kernel trick, and the importance of hyperparameter tuning.

### Chapter 6 – Decision Trees
Introduces decision trees for classification and regression. It explains the CART algorithm, impurity measures such as Gini and entropy, tree regularization, and issues related to instability and overfitting.

### Chapter 7 – Ensemble Learning and Random Forests
Explores ensemble techniques such as voting, bagging, Random Forests, boosting, and stacking. The chapter highlights how combining models improves performance and robustness.

### Chapter 8 – Dimensionality Reduction
Discusses techniques for reducing feature dimensionality while preserving important information. Methods such as PCA, kernel PCA, and manifold learning are covered, along with their use in preprocessing and visualization.

### Chapter 9 – Unsupervised Learning Techniques
Focuses on learning from unlabeled data. Topics include clustering with K-Means and DBSCAN, Gaussian Mixture Models, anomaly detection, and discovering hidden data structures.

### Chapter 10 – Introduction to Artificial Neural Networks with Keras
Introduces neural networks, perceptrons, multilayer perceptrons, and backpropagation. It also demonstrates practical neural network implementation using Keras for regression and classification tasks.

### Chapter 11 – Training Deep Neural Networks
Covers challenges in training deep networks and techniques to address them, including proper initialization, batch normalization, modern optimizers, regularization, dropout, and transfer learning.

### Chapter 12 – Custom Models and Training with TensorFlow
Explains how to build custom models, layers, loss functions, and training loops using TensorFlow. Automatic differentiation and performance optimization using TensorFlow graphs are also discussed.

### Chapter 13 – Loading and Preprocessing Data with TensorFlow
Focuses on building efficient data pipelines using the tf.data API. Topics include batching, prefetching, TFRecord format, preprocessing layers, and scalable data handling.

### Chapter 14 – Deep Computer Vision Using Convolutional Neural Networks
Explores CNNs for computer vision tasks. It covers convolutional and pooling layers, popular CNN architectures, transfer learning, object detection, and semantic segmentation.

### Chapter 15 – Processing Sequences Using RNNs and CNNs
Discusses sequence modeling using RNNs, LSTMs, GRUs, and CNNs. Applications include time-series forecasting and sequence-to-sequence modeling.

### Chapter 16 – Natural Language Processing with RNNs and Attention
Covers NLP tasks such as text generation, sentiment analysis, and machine translation. Encoder–decoder architectures, attention mechanisms, and Transformer models are introduced.

### Chapter 17 – Representation Learning and Generative Learning
Explains autoencoders, variational autoencoders, and generative adversarial networks. The chapter focuses on representation learning and data generation.

### Chapter 18 – Reinforcement Learning
Introduces reinforcement learning concepts including agents, environments, rewards, policies, Markov Decision Processes, Q-learning, Deep Q-Networks, and practical implementations.

### Chapter 19 – Training and Deploying TensorFlow Models at Scale
Focuses on deploying and scaling Machine Learning systems. Topics include TensorFlow Serving, distributed training, GPU utilization, data and model parallelism, and production-level deployment.
